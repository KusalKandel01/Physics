    How the lab works

    An ambulance drives along the road, emitting alternating nee‑naw siren pulses. Every pulse expands outward as a true spherical wavefront at the speed of sound — the ring you see is not attached to the car, exactly as a real pressure wave behaves.

    Because the source keeps moving while each wavefront expands, wavefronts bunch up ahead of the ambulance and stretch out behind it. A stationary listener therefore hears a higher pitch while the ambulance approaches and a lower pitch once it has passed — the Doppler effect.

    Camera

    • Every camera mode is drag-to-look-around and scroll-to-zoom — the buttons just pick your starting framing (chase, orbit, top-down, side).
    • Switching modes resets the view back to that mode's default framing.

    The city

    • Buildings, streetlamps and pedestrians stream in around you as you drive and are recycled behind you, so the town never runs out.
